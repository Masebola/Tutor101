Study the way you already do — just organized

Most students already lean on a friend, a study group, or a senior who explains things well. Tutor 101 doesn't replace that — it just puts it behind your module, so you're not chasing contacts or hoping someone replies to a WhatsApp message the night before a test.

Everything a subscribed module unlocks

Verified tutors

Every tutor is reviewed and approved by an administrator before they can teach a module — no unverified strangers.

Scheduled sessions

Weekly sessions with meeting links appear straight on your module dashboard, including recurring weekly slots.

Notes & past papers

Past exam papers, study guides and notes are organized by module, so you're studying the right material.

Academic support requests

Stuck on one specific thing? Send your tutor a focused request and track it through to an answer.

Ratings & reviews

Only students who actually attended a session can leave a rating, so a tutor's score means something.

Announcements

Tutors post schedule changes and extra sessions straight to everyone enrolled — nothing gets missed.

Sessions that fit around your timetable

Tutors run live sessions over Microsoft Teams, Google Meet or Jitsi. The link only shows up for students who've subscribed to that module, and if a session moves, you'll see the update on your dashboard before it happens — not after.
